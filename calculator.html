Teach it your handwriting

If a digit or letter keeps being misread, pick it here and write it a few times (for letters, write the whole letter — an i with its dot, a t with its bar). Samples are stored only on this device.

Tips

Equations: write the letter x as two crossing strokes. It is read as the variable x when it has a small raised number after it (x²), or nothing to multiply on one side (3x = 5, x + 2 = 7); between two numbers (3 × 4) it stays a multiplication sign. Add = and MathInk solves for x, e.g. 3x² − 7 = 0. If it guesses wrong, tap that symbol and pick × or x.

Functions: write sin, cos, tan, log or ln in small letters (dot the i, cross the t), then the angle, e.g. sin 30 or cos(60). The DEG / RAD button in the top bar picks degrees or radians. If a word isn't recognised, teach the letters below, or tap its first letter and pick the function.

Fractions: draw a flat bar with the top and bottom written above and below it. Roots: draw the √ with its roof over the numbers. Powers: write the exponent small and raised. Division can also be ÷ (bar with two dots). Tap any symbol in the bottom strip to fix a misread.
