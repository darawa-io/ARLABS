Activity · Mathematics · Polyhedrons

        
How many faces?

        
Show the number with your fingers — you can use both hands. A closed fist counts as 10, so 12 is a fist plus two fingers. Press Confirm when ready. Open your right fist to unfold the solid along its edges into a flat net; close it to fold back. Rotate your left hand to spin the model.

Step 5 · Close the loop

        
Feed the result back to the chatbot

        
The first build is never the last one. Point at a complaint to see the re-prompt that fixes it.

        

        
Rule of thumb: describe the behaviour you saw, not the code you think you need.
