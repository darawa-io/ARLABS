Critical Angle θc
The critical angle is the minimum angle of incidence at which Total Internal Reflection occurs.
sin θc = n₂ / n₁
Conditions for TIR:
1. Light travels from a denser medium to a less dense one (n₁ > n₂).
2. The angle of incidence θ₁ must be ≥ θc.
